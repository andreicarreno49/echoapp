How it works

30 seconds a day. Patterns that last.

01

Echo asks

Every day at your chosen time, Echo asks how you feel, what you ate, and how you moved. No apps to download. No forms to fill. Just WhatsApp.

02

Echo remembers

Echo stores your answers and builds a picture of your real patterns — not the ones you think you have, but the ones your data actually shows.

03

Echo reflects

Based on your history, Echo returns one observation — what changed, what repeats, what connects your energy to your habits.
